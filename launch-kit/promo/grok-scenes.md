# Grok video prompts for optional promo b-roll

The main promo (nextgenup-promo.mp4) uses real UI and brand assets.
These prompts generate cinematic b-roll you can splice in front of it
or between scenes for a longer cut. Generate at 16:9. Keep each clip
2 to 4 seconds in the edit.

## Scene 1, the problem (opening hook)
Prompt: Close-up of a person squinting at a laptop screen in a dark
room at night, screen glow on their face, the screen shows a blurry
pixelated video playing, cinematic shallow depth of field, moody blue
and purple lighting, slow push-in.

## Scene 2, pixels
Prompt: Extreme macro shot of a digital screen showing large visible
square pixels in purple and orange sunset colors, camera slowly pulls
back, the pixels gradually merge into a sharp beautiful mountain
sunset image, smooth transition, cinematic.

## Scene 3, transformation
Prompt: A blurry low-resolution photograph lying on a dark desk
magically sharpens into a crystal clear vivid photo, light particles
flowing across its surface from left to right like a scanning line,
dark studio lighting with purple accents, overhead shot.

## Scene 4, the reveal
Prompt: A large bright 4K monitor on a clean dark desk displaying an
ultra sharp colorful sunset mountain landscape, camera orbits slowly
around the desk, purple LED ambient lighting, premium tech commercial
style.

## Scene 5, faces (for the face restoration feature)
Prompt: An old faded damaged black and white family photograph slowly
regains sharpness and detail, a soft light sweep passes over the
faces bringing them into focus, emotional warm tone, close-up, gentle
camera drift.

## Editing notes
- Order: Scene 1, then 2 or 3, then cut to the real promo video
- Add the app footage after the b-roll, never AI-generate the app UI,
  generators hallucinate interface details and it looks fake
- Music: pick an upbeat minimal electronic track (the promo is silent
  by design, social feeds autoplay muted anyway)
- Keep the total under 45 seconds for X and LinkedIn, under 30 is best
